# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, the project's contributors and
maintainers pledge to make participation in the GottLUX project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: using welcoming and inclusive
language, respecting differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive criticism, and
focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information
without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to
the project maintainer ([@Sijon8](https://github.com/Sijon8)) through GitHub — a private security
advisory under the *Security* tab reaches the maintainer without disclosing the report publicly.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainer is
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
